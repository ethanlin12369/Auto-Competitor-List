Gold SERStrate™
Category: SERS Substrates
Enabling ultrasensitive molecular detection down to pM concentrations. Gold SERStrate is an extremely sensitive SERS substrate that enables trace detection of wide range of molecules spanning from life science to military applications.
Gold SERStrate has higher Raman enhancement than any other gold SERS substrate on the market today
Designed for 785 nm laser excitation
Uniform Raman enhancement across the entire substrate
Reproducible enhancement between different substrates and batches enabling quantitative analysis
Low background signal
Most cost effective gold substrate commercially available
Can be used in aggressive solvents and at high temperatures
