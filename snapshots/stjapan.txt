Raman SERS Substrates
Gold Nanorod Array Chips & Slides for Raman signal enhancement and highly reproducible & ultrasensitive measurements
Surface Enhanced Raman Scattering (SERS) is a powerful technique for the detection, identification, quantification and structural studies of minute amounts of samples in materials research, surface science, medical diagnostics, biophysics etc.
Intensity enhancements of approx. 104 up to 106 can be obtained routinely on gold surfaces
Two chips sizes (5 x 7 mm or 3 x 4 mm) as single chips or mounted on glass slides available (ask us for more info)
Excitation Wavelength:
633 nm, 785 nm (recommended), 830 nm
