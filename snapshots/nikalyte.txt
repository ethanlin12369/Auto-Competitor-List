Nikalyte Gold and Silver Surface Enhanced Raman Spectroscopy (SERS) substrates are fabricated in vacuum using our signature gas aggregation technique. In contrast to chemically synthesized nanoparticles, Nikalyte nanoparticles are ultra-pure, and are hydrocarbon free. The paper based substrates are easy to handle and ideally suited for field testing or laboratory use. Simply apply your test analyte and measure immediately, no drying required.
Nikalyte SERS substrates have been tested against Amphetamine sulfate, Caffeine, Fentanyl, BPE, Heroin, MDMA, Acetaminophen, Naproxen, and Amoxicillin. We have supplied SERS substrates to large number of academic and industrial groups globally, working in Healthcare, Pharmaceuticals, Narcotics, Forensics, Environmental Monitoring, Food Safety, in addition to field users working in border control, police force and first responders.

Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is SERS and how does it work?
Surface Enhanced Raman Spectroscopy (SERS) is a technique that amplifies the Raman signal of a molecule when it is near a nanostructured metal surface typically gold or silver. The enhancement arises from localised surface plasmon resonance at the nanoparticle surface, enabling detection of trace analytes at concentrations far below what standard Raman spectroscopy can achieve.
